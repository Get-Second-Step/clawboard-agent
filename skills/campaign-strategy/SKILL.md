# SARASWATI — Campaign Strategy & Planning

> *"Saraswati gives knowledge — the right platform, the right audience, the right message."*

## When to Invoke
- User wants to plan a new campaign from scratch
- User needs platform recommendations for their business type
- User asks "where should I advertise?"
- User wants a launch roadmap
- Prior to audit — strategy context improves audit recommendations

---

## Step 1: Business Discovery

Collect before recommending anything:
- Business type (SaaS / e-commerce / local service / B2B / info product / app)
- Target customer (who buys, what's their pain, where are they)
- Monthly budget range
- Current platforms active (if any)
- Primary goal: awareness / leads / purchases / installs
- Sales cycle length (same day / weeks / months)
- Average order value / lifetime value
- Geographic scope (local / national / global)

---

## Step 2: ICP Definition

Build Ideal Customer Profile:

### Firmographics (B2B)
- Company size, industry, revenue range, tech stack
- Funding stage (bootstrap / funded)

### Demographics (B2C)
- Age, gender, income, location
- Platform behavior (where do they scroll?)

### Psychographics
- Pain intensity (urgent problem vs. nice-to-have)
- Risk tolerance (early adopter vs. conservative)
- Buying triggers (events, seasons, pain points)

### Validation
- Top 20% of customers by LTV — what do they share?
- Fastest sales cycles — what do those customers look like?

## Step 4: Campaign Architecture

### Naming Convention
```
[Platform]_[Objective]_[Audience]_[Geo]_[YYYYQQ]
Examples:
  GOOG_SEARCH_NonBrand_US_2026Q2
  META_CONV_Prospecting_US_2026Q2
  META_CONV_Retargeting_30d_US_2026Q2
```

### Funnel Structure
```
Account
├── Brand (always-on — protect brand terms, low CPC)
├── Prospecting
│   ├── Top Funnel: Awareness (video, display, reach)
│   ├── Mid Funnel: Consideration (intent signals, interest)
│   └── Bottom Funnel: Conversion (high-intent, purchase-ready)
├── Retargeting
│   ├── Website visitors 7 days (hot — close the deal)
│   ├── Website visitors 8–30 days (warm — re-engage)
│   ├── Cart/form abandoners (critical — highest intent)
│   └── Video viewers / engagers (cheap awareness remarketing)
└── Testing (10% budget — always be experimenting)
    └── New audiences, creatives, offers
```

## Step 5: Budget Framework — 70/20/10

| Tier | Allocation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Proven (70%) | Primary platform with proven ROI | Revenue engine |
| Scaling (20%) | Platform showing promise | Growth engine |
| Testing (10%) | New platform or strategy | Innovation budget |

### Budget by Business Stage

**Seed / Early ($1k–$5k/month):**
- 80% one primary platform (usually Google or Meta)
- 20% testing one secondary
- Focus: prove unit economics first

**Growth ($5k–$20k/month):**
- 70% proven primary, 20% secondary, 10% testing
- Start building retargeting audiences

**Scale ($20k+/month):**
- Multi-platform with full funnel
- 70/20/10 across 3+ platforms
- Full measurement infrastructure required

### Budget Pacing Timeline
- Month 1–2: Heavy testing, expect CPA 50% above target (learning)
- Month 3–4: Optimize, tighten — CPA approaching target
- Month 5–6: Scale winners, kill losers
- Ongoing: Quarterly review, 70/20/10 rebalance

## Step 6: Creative Strategy

### 5 Content Pillars
1. **Pain Point** — Directly address the specific problem. "Still manually doing X?"
2. **Social Proof** — Testimonials, case studies, review screenshots, before/afters
3. **Product Demo** — Show it working in context. "Here's exactly how it works"
4. **Offer** — Discount, free trial, lead magnet, guarantee
5. **Education** — Teach something valuable. "3 things top marketers do differently"

### Creative Production Priority
| Priority | Format | Platforms | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Video 15–30s (native feel) | Meta, TikTok, YouTube | 5–10 |
| P2 | Static image with copy overlay | Google, Meta | 10–15 |
| P3 | Carousel (product/benefit each slide) | Meta, LinkedIn | 3–5 |
| P4 | UGC/testimonial video | TikTok, Meta | 3–5 |
| P5 | Long-form (1–3 min explainer) | YouTube | 2–3 |

## Step 7: 90-Day Launch Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
- [ ] Install tracking (Pixel, gtag, CAPI, Consent Mode)
- [ ] Set up conversion events + values
- [ ] Build initial audiences (custom + lookalike)
- [ ] Create campaign architecture + naming
- [ ] Produce first creative batch (P1 + P2 priority)

### Phase 2 — Launch (Weeks 3–4)
- [ ] Launch primary platform only (don't spread budget thin)
- [ ] Conservative bids: Maximize Clicks → Maximize Conversions
- [ ] Monitor daily — verify tracking firing correctly
- [ ] Do NOT edit campaigns in first 7 days (learning phase)

### Phase 3 — Optimize (Weeks 5–8)
- [ ] Analyze data (minimum 2 weeks before decisions)
- [ ] Kill 3x rule: pause any ad group/creative with CPA >3x target
- [ ] Transition to Smart Bidding once ≥30 conversions
- [ ] Launch secondary platform
- [ ] Begin A/B testing: creative, landing pages, audiences

### Phase 4 — Scale (Weeks 9–12)
- [ ] Scale winners by 20% per week (aggressive scaling causes learning phase reset)
- [ ] Expand to testing platform (10% budget)
- [ ] Implement advanced: ABM, PMax, Advantage+
- [ ] Monthly performance review + 90-day planning reset

## Industry Templates

### SaaS
- Primary: Google Search (high-intent "best [category] software")
- Secondary: LinkedIn (if B2B, targeting by job title/company size)
- Conversions: trial_start, demo_request, pricing_page_view
- Attribution: 30-day window (longer sales cycle)
- Key metric: CAC vs LTV ratio

### E-commerce
- Primary: Google Shopping/PMax + Meta (both required)
- Key: Shopping feed quality is everything
- Conversions: purchase, add_to_cart (volume signal), checkout_start
- Attribution: 7-day for most products
- Key metric: ROAS (aim ≥3x ROAS as baseline)

### Local Service (Contractor, Dental, Legal, etc.)
- Primary: Google Search (location + service intent)
- Must have: Call extensions, location extensions, local campaigns
- Conversions: call, form_submit, direction_click
- Budget: ≥$1,500/month to compete in local markets
- Key metric: Cost per call/lead

### B2B Enterprise
- Primary: Google Search + LinkedIn
- LinkedIn targeting: job title, seniority, company size, industry
- Long attribution: 90-day window minimum
- Conversions: demo_request, contact_form, content_download
- Key metric: SQL (sales qualified lead) quality, not just volume
