Common questions
Does Pro still run on my own VPS?
Yes. ClawBoard Pro installs on your Hostinger, DigitalOcean, or any Ubuntu VPS exactly like the open source version. The difference is the license key unlocks multiple clients and all agents. Your data never leaves your server.
What is "managed OAuth" — why does it matter?
In the open source version you need a Google Ads Developer Token to connect accounts — that requires an approved Google API application. With Pro, ClawBoard's OAuth app handles the authorisation. Your clients click one link, approve access, and they're connected. No developer tokens, no API approvals.
What happens if I cancel?
Your license key deactivates and Pro features gate. The open source engine keeps running — Audit Agent, Telegram delivery, and the 1-account limit return. Your reports and credentials stay on your VPS.
How many clients can I manage on Pro?
Unlimited. Switch between accounts instantly from the dashboard. All accounts stay connected simultaneously — no disconnecting one to add another.
Is my client data safe?
Yes. ClawBoard runs on your VPS. No client data passes through any ClawBoard infrastructure — only your license key validation hits our API (a single POST with your key, nothing else). All audit data, reports, and credentials remain on your server behind NemoClaw's guardrails.
