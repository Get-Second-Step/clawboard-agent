🛡️ Security

Most AI tools trust
the model blindly.

ClawBoard Pro does not. Every prompt and every response passes through NemoClaw — a security layer built on NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails — before it touches your ad account data.

🧱
Prompt injection blocked
NemoClaw scans every input for injection patterns before they reach the LLM. Your agent cannot be hijacked by malicious data in ad copy or campaign names.
✅
Output validation
Every response is checked before it reaches your report or delivery channel. Hallucinated figures and fabricated data are caught and blocked.
🔒
Credentials never leave your VPS
OAuth tokens, API keys, and client account IDs are stored only in your VPS. ClawBoard Pro has no cloud component that touches your credentials.
🏠
Self-hosted by design
No SaaS vendor sees your client data. No shared infrastructure. Your accounts run in isolation on your own server, on your network.
